# Human quiz review guide

## Lecturer review

Focus on correctness, concept alignment, difficulty appropriateness, distractor quality, explanation correctness, citation trust, and the publish decision. Lecturer review has greater authority for technical correctness.

## Student review

Focus on clarity, perceived difficulty, usefulness, whether the explanation helps, and whether source navigation is useful.

Use only `YES`, `EDIT`, or `REJECT` for `publish_decision`. Leave scores blank until review occurs.
